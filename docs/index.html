Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use my existing computer and printer?

Yes! BizFlow software runs on almost any standard Windows laptop or desktop (Windows 7/8/10/11) and works seamlessly with USB and Bluetooth thermal printers.

What happens if our shop internet goes down?

Our POS and billing software works 100% offline. You can continue scanning items, accepting cash/UPI payments, and printing bills uninterrupted.

Do you help us add all our shop items and prices?

Yes. If you have an Excel sheet or handwritten list of your items, we help upload them so your counter is ready to bill on day one.
